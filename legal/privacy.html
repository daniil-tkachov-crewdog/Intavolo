Privacy Policy

Intavolo · Effective 23 November 2026

Who we are

Intavolo is an app for learning Italian. Intavolo is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: intavolo.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the account identifier that provider gives us. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only receive Apple's relay address.
  • Learning progress: the words you add, their learning status, review dates, daily exercise counts, daily goal and streak data.
  • Password: if you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. We never see it.

In guest mode, nothing is sent to us. Your progress stays on your device only.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Data that stays on your device

  • App settings (theme, speech speed, reminder time) and grammar lesson scores.
  • Daily reminders are scheduled locally by your device. No push server is involved.
  • Pronunciation audio is produced by your device's built-in text-to-speech. No text is sent to us for this.

Why we use it

We use your data only to provide the app: to sign you in and to save and sync your learning progress across your devices. The legal basis under UK GDPR is the performance of our contract with you (providing the service you asked for).

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database and authentication.
  • Apple and Google, only if you choose to sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers our account emails, such as confirmation and password reset.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress until you delete your account. You can do this at any time in the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently erases your account and all synced progress from our servers. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and you can object to or restrict its processing. To do this, email intavolo.app@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Intavolo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to your progress is restricted to your own account.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top, and we will tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
