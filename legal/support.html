Common questions

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. You will get a link to set a new password.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam folder. The email comes from Intavolo (intavolo.app@gmail.com).

Is my progress saved?

With an account, your progress syncs automatically and is restored when you log in on another device. To sync manually, go to Profile → Account → Sync now. In guest mode, progress is saved on your device only.

Reminders don't arrive

Turn on notifications for Intavolo in iOS Settings → Notifications → Intavolo, then enable Daily reminder on the Streak screen.

I can't hear the pronunciation

Turn up the volume and check the silent switch. Make sure an Italian voice is installed: iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Italian.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Account → Delete account. Your account and all synced progress are permanently deleted.
